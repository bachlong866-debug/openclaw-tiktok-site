Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026 · Applies to this informational website

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how the informational website for OpenClaw TikTok Uploader (the “Site”) handles personal information at its current development stage. It does not claim that a working TikTok integration exists. It does not cover separate local development tools that are not publicly offered through the Site.

2. Information collected directly by this Site

The Site does not provide user accounts, contact forms, TikTok login, media upload, analytics scripts or advertising trackers. Its pages do not intentionally set first-party tracking cookies. The Site itself does not request your TikTok account information, videos, OAuth tokens, passwords or other credentials.

3. Information processed by hosting infrastructure

The Site may be hosted using GitHub Pages. Like other website hosts, the hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, browser or device information, time of access, and requested pages, as needed for hosting, security and infrastructure operation. These activities are governed by the hosting provider’s own notices and policies. The operator does not currently run a separate visitor analytics system.

4. Contact by email

If you choose to contact the project operator by email, the email provider and operator will process the name, email address and information you provide to respond to your message. Do not include account passwords, access tokens, sensitive documents or unnecessary personal information. Messages will be retained only as reasonably needed to respond, handle related follow-up requests or satisfy legal obligations.

5. TikTok and other third-party APIs

The Site currently does not connect to TikTok, use TikTok OAuth, receive TikTok profile data, upload videos to TikTok or store TikTok access tokens. Separate development tests have used authorized YouTube and Facebook API connections, but those functions are not exposed through this Site. Before any such features become part of a public service, this policy will be updated to specify the precise data requested, usage, retention, access, deletion and disconnection procedures.

6. Sharing

The operator does not intentionally sell visitor personal information. Information provided through the hosting or email infrastructure may be processed by those service providers to deliver their services. No third-party advertising integrations are included in the current Site.

7. Security

No website can guarantee absolute security. The Site does not ask visitors to send credentials. Please do not submit tokens, passwords or other secrets through email or links associated with this project.

8. Your requests

To request information about personal information you sent directly to the project operator, or to request correction or deletion where applicable, contact bachlong866@gmail.com. For technical data processed independently by the hosting provider, see that provider’s privacy notice and support channels.

9. Children and sensitive information

The Site is not designed to solicit children’s personal information or sensitive personal data. Please do not share such information with the operator through this Site.

10. Changes to this policy

The operator will update this policy before activating materially different functionality, especially authentication, analytics, token storage or video uploading. The effective date will be updated accordingly.

Notice to operator: Replace the contact email and confirm that the actual hosting, data practices and retention match this policy before publishing. This is a project-specific starter draft, not legal advice.
